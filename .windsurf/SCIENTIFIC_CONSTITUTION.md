# ============================================================
# QUANTA SCIENTIFIC CONSTITUTION
# Version 1.0
# ============================================================

This document defines the immutable scientific principles of QUANTA.

It does not describe implementation.

It defines scientific law.

No software architecture, optimization, artificial intelligence model,
or engineering decision may violate these principles.

============================================================
PREAMBLE
============================================================

Science is built on evidence.

Evidence is built on reproducibility.

Reproducibility is built on transparency.

Therefore,

QUANTA exists to protect scientific integrity.

Every analysis generated by QUANTA shall prioritize scientific truth over convenience, automation or commercial interests.

============================================================
ARTICLE I
THE PRIMACY OF EVIDENCE
============================================================

Evidence always precedes interpretation.

Numbers create conclusions.

Language explains conclusions.

Evidence shall never be generated by probabilistic models.

Only deterministic statistical computation may produce evidence.

Artificial Intelligence shall never become a source of evidence.

============================================================
ARTICLE II
DETERMINISTIC COMPUTATION
============================================================

All statistical calculations must be deterministic.

Given the same:

dataset

parameters

configuration

software version

the result must always be identical.

Randomized procedures shall declare:

algorithm

seed

reproducibility conditions

============================================================
ARTICLE III
REPRODUCIBILITY
============================================================

Every scientific result generated by QUANTA must be independently reproducible.

Each report shall include:

analysis identifier

software version

execution date

methodology

statistical assumptions

reproduction scripts

No result shall depend upon hidden state.

============================================================
ARTICLE IV
TRANSPARENCY
============================================================

Nothing important shall remain hidden.

Every transformation must be documented.

Every exclusion must be justified.

Every warning must be visible.

Every assumption must be explicit.

Silence is prohibited.

============================================================
ARTICLE V
TRACEABILITY
============================================================

Every analytical decision must leave an audit trail.

Audit logs shall include:

selected statistical test

reason for selection

diagnostic results

data transformations

warnings

execution order

A scientist must be able to reconstruct the complete reasoning process.

============================================================
ARTICLE VI
STATISTICAL HONESTY
============================================================

QUANTA shall never exaggerate certainty.

The system shall explicitly report:

limitations

violated assumptions

small samples

missing data

weak effect sizes

low statistical power

high uncertainty

Confidence shall never be overstated.

============================================================
ARTICLE VII
THE RIGHTS OF DATA
============================================================

Every observation deserves justification.

Rows shall never be silently removed.

Missing values shall never be silently imputed.

Outliers shall never be silently ignored.

Transformations require documentation.

The original dataset shall remain recoverable.

============================================================
ARTICLE VIII
THE RIGHTS OF THE RESEARCHER
============================================================

Researchers have the right to understand every conclusion.

Researchers have the right to reproduce every analysis.

Researchers have the right to challenge every recommendation.

Researchers shall never be forced to trust a black box.

============================================================
ARTICLE IX
THE ROLE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
============================================================

Artificial Intelligence is an interpreter.

Never a statistician.

Never a calculator.

Never a scientific authority.

The LLM shall:

translate

summarize

explain

contextualize

It shall never:

compute

estimate

fabricate

invent significance

invent methodology

modify statistical evidence.

============================================================
ARTICLE X
METHODOLOGICAL JUSTIFICATION
============================================================

Every statistical method must answer three questions.

Why was this method selected?

Why were alternative methods rejected?

What assumptions support this decision?

Method selection without justification is invalid.

============================================================
ARTICLE XI
ROBUSTNESS
============================================================

Scientific conclusions should resist criticism.

Future versions of QUANTA shall progressively verify:

bootstrap stability

alternative statistical tests

sensitivity analysis

cross-validation

robust estimators

counterfactual analysis

Whenever possible,

QUANTA should attempt to invalidate its own conclusions before presenting them.

============================================================
ARTICLE XII
REPORTING
============================================================

Reports generated by QUANTA shall be:

clear

honest

complete

reproducible

professionally formatted

Every conclusion must distinguish between:

observation

interpretation

recommendation

These concepts shall never be confused.

============================================================
ARTICLE XIII
SCIENTIFIC LANGUAGE
============================================================

Language must remain scientifically rigorous.

Forbidden expressions include:

"This proves..."

"It is certain..."

"There is no doubt..."

Preferred expressions include:

"The results suggest..."

"The evidence supports..."

"Within the limitations..."

"The observed association..."

Precision is mandatory.

============================================================
ARTICLE XIV
CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
============================================================

Every scientific criticism strengthens QUANTA.

Every detected limitation shall become a future improvement.

Every reproducibility issue shall become a permanent test.

Scientific evolution never ends.

============================================================
ARTICLE XV
THE THREE JUDGES
============================================================

Every analysis must survive independent evaluation by:

1.
A Senior Statistician

2.
A Thesis Committee

3.
A Scientific Reviewer

Failure before any of these judges constitutes failure of the analysis itself.

============================================================
ARTICLE XVI
THE QUANTA OATH
============================================================

QUANTA shall never sacrifice scientific integrity for:

speed

simplicity

marketing

automation

user satisfaction

commercial pressure

Truth remains the highest authority.

============================================================
FINAL DECLARATION
============================================================

QUANTA is not built to automate statistics.

QUANTA is built to preserve scientific trust.

Every line of code.

Every statistical calculation.

Every report.

Every future version.

Shall remain faithful to this Constitution.

============================================================
END OF DOCUMENT
